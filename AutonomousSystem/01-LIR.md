# Sponsoring LIR decision

From the beginning i considered two LIRs, [iFog GmbH](https://ifog.ch) and [Lagrange Cloud](https://lagrange.cloud).
I sent emails to both iFog and Lagrange with questions regarding, if my planned upstreams qualify, RIPE fees, billing periods, and announcing their PAs on third-party VPSes.

Final confirmed costs turned out to be, for iFog 77 CHF a year and that includes an ASN plus a PA /48 IPv6, and for Lagrange 54,99GBP a year for the ASN and the PA /48 is free for the first year, then 4 GBP a year.   
Lagrange also provides a self-service panel for managing IRR, RPKI, LOA, WHOIS and rDNS. They also stated in an email that they discourage the use of LOAs.   

So the costs are fairly the same. iFog's 77CHF a year is right now ~356 PLN, and Lagrange's 54,99GBP is ~278PLN for the first year and around 298PLN for every next year.    

In full honesty, I really started liking iFog to some extent cause they are from Switzerland, and I like Switzerland in general, I have been there, though I know that is not really a super important thing in terms of deciding on which LIR to choose. 
Also they offer speeds up to 10Gbps on their VPSes.

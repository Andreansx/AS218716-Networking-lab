# Sponsoring LIR decision

From the beginning i considered two LIRs, [iFog GmbH](https://ifog.ch) and [Lagrange Cloud](https://lagrange.cloud).
I sent emails to both iFog and Lagrange with questions regarding, if my planned upstreams qualify, RIPE fees, billing periods, and announcing their PAs on third-party VPSes.

Final confirmed costs turned out to be, for iFog 77 CHF a year and that includes an ASN plus a PA /48 IPv6, and for Lagrange 54,99GBP a year for the ASN and the PA /48 is free for the first year, then 4 GBP a year.   
Lagrange also provides a self-service panel for managing IRR, RPKI, LOA, WHOIS and rDNS. They also stated in an email that they discourage the use of LOAs.   

So the costs are fairly the same. iFog's 77CHF a year is right now ~356 PLN, and Lagrange's 54,99GBP is ~278PLN for the first year and around 298PLN for every next year.    

In full honesty, I really started liking iFog to some extent cause they are from Switzerland, and I like Switzerland in general, I have been there, though I know that is not really a super important thing in terms of deciding on which LIR to choose. 
Also they offer speeds up to 10Gbps on their VPSes.   

### 1st of September 2026

I have submit an application for ASN + PA /48 to Lagrange Cloud. As for now the application is awaiting staff review, but the Pa /48 block has been allocated already.   
iFog has responded to my email and my ticket literally like 3 minutes after I paid for the ASN from Lagrange Cloud, but that is not really a problem, as iFog will still be one of my upstreams, cause I have a VPS there.   
But now roles are really granulated, I mean one VPS is from Servperso Systems, the second one is from iFog GmbH and the ASN and PA /48 is from Lagrange Cloud, so no company controls more than one thing.   

As for the ASN, I paid 86.09 GBP, and that includes the initiation fee for the ASN of 15 GBP, the RIPE annual fee of 54.99 GBP and PL VAT of 23% which came out to 16.10 GBP.   

One thing to note here is that at the checkout there was an option "Pay this year's annual fee upfront instead of in November and get £5 of free credit to spend on Lagrange products.", and this is a good option so I checked the box near it. 
This answers one question, specifically about their billing periods. They bill every year at 1st of November, so by paying now I have acquired 14 months of ASN + PA /48 ownership for the same price as I would normally pay for 12 months (or at least it seems so, cause the renew is set to be on 1st Nov 2027).   

Lagrange Cloud has a really nice tutorial that guided me through the setup in RIPE Database, how to create maintainer+person pair, how to create org object etc.
After that there was KYC check via Yoti. I was worried that I didn't have their application installed (cause I thought I should have some app installed), but it all went through the browser. 
I submitted my passport for the document.   
Of course I had to enter the ASNs and NOC emails of my two upstreams, which are iFog GmbH and Servperso Systems.
